# Short retention rules (news + money)

Use this whenever you write or rebuild a daily Short. Spoken words stay English. Facts stay sourced. Hooks must never invent numbers, seats, polls, or prices.

Adapted for this channel from public short-form craft:

- Spotlight-Revenue / short-form-video-script-writer (hooks, re-hooks, one idea, open loops)
- xsrazy / fyp-skills video-hook (pattern families: result first, mistake, curiosity gap, contrast, numbered promise)
- ReelsBot / ViralMint research: first word ASAP; median hit Shorts ~21s; no logo/greeting before the point
- HookSignals psychology: curiosity gap, pattern interrupt, relevance signal, payoff promise
- News clip templates: 0–3s why-care hook → facts → why it matters → CTA

## What the viewer decides

| Second | Viewer question | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Is this for me? | Lead photo + big on-screen hook text (works muted) |
| 1–3 | Why stay? | Spoken hook names the top story or the money number |
| 5–7 | Still worth it? | Re-hook: why it matters / what the number means |
| Rest | Payoff | Sourced facts only; close the loop; CTA; disclaimer |

## News Short structure

1. **Rank stories first.** Score by: two outlets, hot words (election, war, Fed, storm…), digits in title, clarity. Put the highest score first. Never pick the first three RSS items by luck.
2. **Hook (spoken + on screen).** One line, ≤12 words on screen. Patterns allowed: "Start with this: {lead}." / "The story U.S. outlets are chasing: {lead}." / "Today's top U.S. story: {lead}."
3. **Re-hook.** One sentence: why the lead story hits votes, markets, safety, or paychecks. No new invented facts.
4. **Lead story.** Headline + one summary sentence + outlet. Label it lead.
5. **Two supporting stories.** Shorter. Keep photos credited.
6. **Close.** "That's today's U.S. briefing. Follow for tomorrow's top story." + not-advice line.

Trim order when over 58s: drop briefs → drop lowest-ranked story → never drop hook/re-hook.

## Money Short structure

1. **Proof-first hook.** "If you keep one money number this week, make it {number}."
2. **Re-hook.** First fact sentence: what the number is.
3. **One contrast or context beat.** What it includes / excludes, or the arithmetic.
4. **Where the example fails.** Honest limit builds trust.
5. **This week.** One concrete action.
6. **Disclaimer.** "This is not personal financial advice."

One idea only. Title must keep a digit. Source stays on screen.

## On-screen text

- First frame must carry the hook without sound.
- Captions: ≤2 lines, ≥56 px, word highlight on the spoken token.
- Never put text in the YouTube chrome zones (top ~230px, bottom from ~1500px, right button column below mid).
- Every drawn box is logged in `short-*.layout.json` and re-measured by `kiem_dinh.py`.

## Forbidden

- "Hey guys", logo card, empty breath before the first word
- Catchy lines that change the outlet's fact
- Clickbait the video cannot pay off
- Listing three ideas in one Short
- Cutting a Short out of a 1280×720 long video

## Competitor compare (before claiming better)

For each high-view rival Short, note:

1. Opening frame (face / photo / graphic / text)
2. First spoken 3 seconds
3. Whether a re-hook appears by ~7s
4. Duration and estimated completion pressure (shorter usually wins)
5. Caption size and safe-zone placement
6. What loop opened and when it closed
7. CTA type

Our Short must beat the rival on at least two of: clearer lead story, faster first word, bigger muted hook text, tighter duration, or a closed loop with a sourced payoff. Record the notes in the day folder or in `doi_thu_phan_tich.json`.
